Video formats & tips
Accepted video formats: MP4 (H.264) works everywhere. MOV and WebM work on most phones and browsers. HEVC (H.265), AVI, MKV and WMV often do not. Tip: film a few seconds in 1080p or lower.
Film perpendicular to the plane of motion, with the ruler in that same plane; otherwise measurements are distorted.
